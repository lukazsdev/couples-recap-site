What the app does

Moved On finds the photos and videos of a person you choose in your photo library, and lets you delete or hide them. How it handles your photos and data is described in the privacy policy, which is part of these terms.

Your photos, your decision

  • The app only changes your photo library when you tell it to, and iOS asks you to confirm every change. What you delete or hide is your decision.
  • Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted, where iOS keeps them for 30 days before removing them for good. After that, or after you empty Recently Deleted yourself, they cannot be brought back by us or by the app.
  • Face matching is done by software and is not perfect: it can miss photos, and it can include a photo of someone who looks alike. Check what the app has chosen before you delete it.
  • Keep your own copies of any photos you may want later.

Using it fairly

Use the app for your own photo library. Do not use it to look through someone else's phone or photos without their permission.
