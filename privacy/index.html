Moved On

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Moved On is an iPhone app that finds the photos and videos of one person (usually an ex) in your photo library, so you can delete or hide them. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it.

Moved On is made by Velori Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17529890) ("we", "us"). Questions: lukazsdev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Everything happens on your iPhone. Your photos, the faces in them and what the app finds are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account. We do not know who you are, or who you are looking for.
  • Nothing is deleted without you. Every change to your photo library is confirmed by you, in iOS's own window, first.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we do not sell data. The only data that leaves your phone is what Apple and our payment provider need when you buy Premium.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photo library

With your permission the app reads your photo library. To find out who shows up in your photos it looks at a sample of them, finds the faces with Apple's on-device Vision framework, and shows you the people who appear most, so you can pick one. It then goes through your photos and videos (for a video, a few of its frames) looking for that face. The photos and videos that do not show them are ignored.

Face data

To recognise a face the app turns it into a list of numbers (a face "embedding"). The embeddings of the person you picked (and of you, if you picked yourself too) and small pictures of their faces are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. They are never uploaded, never shared and never used for anything else. They are deleted when you choose to look for someone else, when you erase everything in Settings, or when you delete the app.

What the app finds and remembers

A list of the photos and videos that show the person (references to them in your library, their dates and sizes, and whether you were in them too), which ones you chose to keep, which ones the app hid for you, the date you started moving on, how many times you used "I want to text them", and the reasons you write down. All of it stays in the app on your iPhone, and all of it goes when you erase everything in Settings or delete the app.

Changes to your photo library

The app deletes or hides photos only when you ask it to, and iOS asks you to confirm each time. Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album, where iOS keeps them for 30 days. Hidden photos go to the Hidden album, which iOS locks with Face ID; you can bring them back from the app's Settings. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple applies the same change on your other devices.

Share cards

If you tap Share, the app makes a picture on your phone with the number of photos removed and the app's name (never a photo of anyone) and hands it to iOS's share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Notifications

If you allow them, milestone notifications ("One week.") are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them or knows when they are due.

What leaves your iPhone

When you buy Premium

Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card or your Apple ID. To know whether you have Premium, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives from Apple your purchase history for this app (which product, when, the price and currency) under a random ID it creates for this app. That ID is not linked to your name or to anything in your photos.

Usage statistics

This version of Moved On does not collect usage statistics or analytics of any kind.

Crash reports

If you have agreed in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They do not contain your photos.

If you used Couples Recap

Moved On is a new version of the app that was called Couples Recap. When it first opens, it erases what Couples Recap kept on your phone (its settings, its face data and its reminders). If you used Couples Recap to share photos with a partner, copies of the photos you shared are still on our servers (hosted by Supabase). Email us and we will delete everything we hold about you within 30 days.

Who else handles the data

  • Apple provides the photo library, the face detection that runs on your phone, iCloud Photos if you use it, notifications and payments.
  • RevenueCat manages purchases as described above. It is based in the United States.

We do not use advertising networks, and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

Why we are allowed to use it

Under UK and EU data protection law: reading your photo library and matching faces happen on your device with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings; purchase data is used to perform our contract with you (giving you the Premium you bought). We never receive face data, so we do not process biometric data at all.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until you erase it in Settings or delete the app.
  • At RevenueCat: purchase records, for as long as needed for your purchases and for tax and accounting rules.

Your choices and rights

  • Look for someone else: Settings → Look for someone else.
  • Erase everything the app knows: Settings → Erase everything.
  • Bring hidden photos back: Settings → Bring back hidden.
  • Remove photo access: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

You also have the right to ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to a regulator. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Email us and we will reply within one month.

Age

Moved On is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change what the app collects or who handles it, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
