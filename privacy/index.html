Couples Wrapped

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Couples Wrapped is an iPhone app for couples. It finds the photos of the two of you in your photo library, puts both partners' photos on one shared timeline, and makes a recap of your year. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it.

Couples Wrapped is made by Velori Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17529890), which is responsible for this data ("we", "us"). Questions: lukazsdev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your photos are looked through on your iPhone. They are not sent to us to be analysed.
  • Face matching never leaves your device. We never receive face data, and we could not hand it to anyone.
  • Nothing is uploaded until you say so. Your partner sees your photos only if you choose to share them, and you can leave any photo out.
  • You can undo it. Stop sharing and your uploads are deleted. Delete your account and everything we hold about you goes with it.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, and we do not sell data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your photo library

With your permission the app reads your photo library to find photos with the two of you in them. It looks at the pictures, when they were taken and where (if the photo has a location). Photos that do not match are ignored: nothing about them is kept. The app never changes or deletes photos in your library. It adds to your library only when you save a recap video.

Face data

To recognise you and your partner the app creates a mathematical description of each of your two faces, from photos you pick during setup. This is face data, sometimes called biometric data. It is created on your iPhone, stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone, left out of device backups, and used only to find photos of the two of you. It is never uploaded, never shared with your partner and never sent to us or anyone else. The small face pictures shown while you choose "which one is your partner" are kept in a temporary cache on your iPhone.

You can delete this at any time in Settings → Faces and scanning → Delete face data. Deleting the app deletes it too.

Because the app learns your partner's face from your photos, only use it with a partner who is happy for you to do so.

Everything else the app keeps on your phone

The list of matched photos (which photo, when, where, and who is in it), the names you typed, the day you got together, your recaps, your settings, and your anniversary reminders, which are scheduled by your iPhone and not by a server.

What is sent to Apple to name places

To turn a photo's coordinates into a place name ("Lisbon") and to draw the map in your recap, your iPhone asks Apple's Maps service. Apple receives approximate coordinates of the places, not your photos and not who is in them. Apple's handling of that is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What is sent to us

Nothing is sent to us until you create or join a couple. From then on:

An account

The app creates an account with a random ID. It has no name or email attached. If you choose "Save with Apple", we receive the identifier Apple gives the app for you and the email address Apple shares (which can be Apple's private relay address), so you can get back into your account on a new phone.

Your couple

The first name you entered, the day you got together if you gave one, your invite codes, and the number of photos of the two of you your phone found (the number only). Your partner sees your first name and that number.

Photos you choose to share

If you choose to share, the app uploads copies of your matched photos so your partner can see them. Location data is removed from the image files themselves. With each photo we store when it was taken, its coordinates and place name if it had a location, its size, whether it shows both of you or one of you, whether it is a selfie, whether you marked it as a favourite, and a scrambled reference that lets your own phone recognise the photo again (it means nothing to anyone else). Only you and your partner can see them. Free accounts upload a reduced-size copy; premium accounts upload the full-size photo as a backup.

You can leave any photo out before or after sharing, and you can stop sharing at any time, which deletes your uploads.

Recaps

The numbers and place names in a recap (days together, photo counts, places, dates) are saved to your couple so you both see the same recap.

Subscription status

If you subscribe, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details. RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us, tells us whether your subscription is active, which plan it is and when it ends.

Photo books

Ordering a printed photo book is not open yet. When it opens, we will need a delivery name, address and email, and the photos in the book will be sent to the printer. We will update this policy before that happens.

Who else handles the data

  • Supabase stores the account, couple and shared-photo data described above on our behalf, in data centres in the United Kingdom.
  • RevenueCat manages subscriptions and receives your random account ID and purchase history from Apple. It is based in the United States.
  • Apple provides sign-in, payments, maps and place names as described above.

We do not use advertising networks or analytics companies, and we do not sell or rent data to anyone.

Why we are allowed to use it

Under UK and EU data protection law we rely on: your consent for reading your photo library, for face matching on your device and for sharing photos with your partner (you can withdraw it at any time in the app or in iOS Settings); and the contract between us for running your account, your couple and your subscription. We never receive face data, so we do not process biometric data on our servers.

How long we keep it

  • Shared photos and couple data: until you stop sharing, leave, or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your account, your membership, your photo records and your uploaded photos are deleted. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
  • Your partner keeps their own photos and their own account when you leave or delete yours.

Archive mode

If a relationship ends you can archive it in Settings. Everything is hidden on your phone and reminders stop. You can also hide your shared photos from your former partner at the same time. Nothing is deleted until you choose "Delete everything permanently", and your former partner is not told that you archived.

Your choices and rights

  • See what the app has: your timeline shows every photo it matched.
  • Leave a photo out: open it and switch off "Include".
  • Stop sharing: Settings → What I share.
  • Delete face data: Settings → Faces and scanning.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → You two → Delete account and data.
  • Remove photo access: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

You also have the right to ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, to correct it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to a regulator. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Email us and we will reply within one month.

Age

Couples Wrapped is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change what the app collects or who handles it, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
